Adopt, Foster & Volunteer

Whether you want to bring a pet home, open your home temporarily, or give your time, there is a way for you to change an animal's life.

Adoption Process

Meet animals like Peanut, one of the many pets currently waiting for a forever home. Our adoption process is simple and supportive:

  1. Browse and apply: look through our adoptable animals and fill out the interest form on our Get Involved page.
  2. Meet and greet: visit the rescue to spend time with the animal and make sure it is a good fit for your household.
  3. Home check: we do a quick home visit to confirm a safe environment for the animal.
  4. Adoption day: finalize the paperwork and take your new family member home.
  5. Follow-up support: we check in after adoption and are always here to help with the transition.

Foster Program

Fosters give animals a comfortable home environment while they wait to be adopted. It is one of the most direct ways to save a life.

  • We cover all food, supplies, and medical costs.
  • Foster periods usually range from two weeks to three months.
  • You choose the type of animal that fits your home and lifestyle.
  • Our foster coordinator supports you every step of the way.

Volunteer Roles

Our volunteers keep the rescue running. Current roles include:

  • Dog walker: give our dogs exercise, fresh air, and social time.
  • Cat socializer: help shy cats build trust and confidence around people.
  • Event helper: represent the rescue at adoption events and community fairs.
  • Transport driver: drive animals to vet appointments and foster homes.
  • Administrative support: assist with applications, emails, and records.

Giving Twin Cities Animals a Second Chance

Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community nonprofit serving Minneapolis and St. Paul. We rescue abandoned and surrendered animals, give them medical care and a safe place to stay, and match them with loving forever homes. We believe every animal deserves compassion, and every family deserves the joy a pet brings.

Our Impact

Thanks to our volunteers, fosters, and donors, our rescue keeps growing:

  • Over 1,200 animals rescued since 2015
  • More than 950 successful adoptions
  • 150 active volunteers and foster families
  • 40 community partner organizations across the Twin Cities

How You Can Help

There is a place for everyone at Twin Cities Animal Rescue:

  • Adopt: give a rescued animal a permanent, loving home.
  • Foster: care for an animal short-term while they wait for adoption.
  • Volunteer: help with dog walking, cat socializing, and community events.
  • Donate: support food, shelter, and medical care for animals in need.

Ready to take the first step? Fill out our interest form and we will be in touch.

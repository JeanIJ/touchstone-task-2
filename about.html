About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2015 when a small group of neighbors in Minneapolis started taking in stray cats found near the Greenway. What started as three foster homes and a shared spreadsheet has grown into a community nonprofit with a dedicated shelter space, 150 active volunteers, and partnerships across Minneapolis and St. Paul.

In our first year we placed 40 animals in new homes. Today, we have rescued more than 1,200 animals, from dogs and cats to rabbits and guinea pigs. Every one of them arrived with a story, and every adoption writes a happy next chapter.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion first: every animal is treated with patience and kindness, no matter their background.
  • Community powered: volunteers, fosters, and donors make everything we do possible.
  • Trust and transparency: we are open about our process, our finances, and the care each animal receives.
  • Lifelong commitment: our support does not end on adoption day. We are here for the life of every animal we place.

Our Partners

We could not do this work alone. Twin Cities Animal Rescue is grateful for the support of our community partners:

  • River Road Veterinary Clinic, providing discounted medical care
  • North Loop Pet Supply, donating food and litter every month
  • Twin Cities Parks Alliance, hosting our adoption events
  • Hennepin County Libraries, sharing our foster program with the community
